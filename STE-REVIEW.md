# Controlled English review: new agent and publisher features

Date: 2026-10-14
Reference: ASD-STE100 Issue 9, January 2025.

Scope: the three new feature guides, the replacement architecture chapter,
and the new sections in the home page, integrations, API reference and glossary.
Existing chapters and translations are outside this review.

The review uses the writing rules and dictionary in the locally available
standard. The standard itself is not copied into this repository.

- Instructions use active voice and numbered steps with one principal action.
- Instruction sentences have at most 20 words. Descriptive sentences have at
  most 25 words. Paragraphs have at most six sentences on one subject.
- The text uses explicit conditions, consistent names and literal UI/API names.
- Computer and advertising terms have defined meanings in the glossary.
- Computer verbs such as configure, install, save, revoke and hash refer only
  to their stated technical operations. Use approved general verbs where
  they express the same meaning.
- Commands, paths, identifiers and literal UI labels retain their exact form.
- A local sentence-length scan supplements the manual vocabulary and meaning
  review. It is not a complete automated conformity test or certification.

The guides describe the deployed MCP/API and the existing private Grok
connection separately from the reusable bridge in the release candidate.
They do not claim a stable release, saved publisher directory, verified
management mandate from DIRECT entries, or automatic spend qualification.

The new guides are English. Other locales use the configured English fallback
until translations exist. Navigation uses normal page loads because the
localization plugin reports that instant navigation is incompatible with its
contextual language switcher.
